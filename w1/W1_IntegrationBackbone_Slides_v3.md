WHERE DATA IS BORN
Control points: where the business commits
• Trace either flow and a few moments matter far more than the rest: a PO is issued, a serial or lot number generated, credit
approved, an invoice posted.
• These are control points — the instants where the business commits and a new, authoritative fact comes into existence.
• They are where governance attaches, where an error is cheapest to catch, and — later in the course — where automation
and AI agents plug in.
• So we read the process before the data. Data hangs on a process, not the other way round. Business first, data last.
A control point is where data is born. Find them first: in Lab 1 you'll mark them on your own process map. Map the business process
and its control points first; fit the data to it last.

How to spot a company that lacks a foundation
• Different parts of the company give different answers to the same customer question.
• Meeting a new regulation is a massive, top-driven scramble.
• Every new initiative feels like starting from scratch.
• IT is the perpetual bottleneck — and people spend their days moving data from one system into another.
Learn to hear these in the wild — in a case, an interview, your own employer. Each is evidence the foundation is weak, whatever
the software cost. Westerman's study of 400+ large firms found the winners aren't the biggest spenders — the “Digital Masters” run
