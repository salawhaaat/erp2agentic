### The backbone every company runs on — your Week 1 read is live
Aug 26, 2026 4:14 AM
Hi everyone,

We're one week out, and I'm genuinely excited for next Friday. The Week 1 study note is now posted on Brightspace — the first piece of the story we'll build together all term.

Here's the idea it opens on. Every company generates far more information than any person could hold in their head, and most organizations keep that information scattered across dozens of disconnected systems. A company with fragmented systems is, in a very real sense, a fragmented company — its parts literally cannot see each other. The whole course starts from the thing that fixes this: the integration backbone, the single source of truth an enterprise runs on. Get that right and everything downstream — automation, AI, agents — becomes possible. Miss it, and the smartest technology in the world has nothing solid to stand on. That's Week 1.

A quick word on how reading works here, because it's a little different. The study note is the main text — the primary reading you do before each week's class, not a supplement to a textbook. The note carries the teaching; a short real-world case each week is your applied reading; the books are there when you want to go deeper.

I'd encourage you to read the Week 1 note before Friday. Our first class is built to bring everyone up to speed no matter what — but coming in having read it will put you a step ahead and let you get more out of our time together.
The Week 0 primer is there for you too, and it's especially worth a look if some of the fundamentals are new to you — what an ERP actually is, how the pieces of an enterprise fit together, and how to read a business process (BPMN). It's optional, and we'll cover what you need in class regardless — but if you're coming in without much background here, a little time with Week 0 Course Primer now is the surest way to start the term on solid footing.
After this week, the rhythm settles in: you'll get each note a full week ahead, and reading before class is the habit that will serve you best. Week 1 is the gentle on-ramp — a lighter lift, because we're just getting going.
