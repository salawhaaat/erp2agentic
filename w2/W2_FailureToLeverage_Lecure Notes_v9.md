Locate the leak
Take one end-to-end process on your company's backbone — Order-to-Cash or Procure-to-Pay — and run the Four Lenses over it.
WORK THROUGH IT, IN ORDER
1 Sketch the process end to end — a few boxes, from the first trigger to “done,” is enough.
2 Walk it with each lens in turn — Frustration, Time, Cost, Quality — and note at least one value leak under each.
3 Tag every leak with the thread it rings: Customer, Operations, or Cash.
4 Circle the overlap — the leak that shows up under two or three lenses. That is where one fix pays three times.
Time
Output A Four-Lenses friction list for one process. 12 min
+ 2 debrief

BREAKOUT 2 · 9:13–9:37
9:13
Obliterate — selectively
From your friction list, decide where to redesign — and, just as important, where to leave things alone.
WORK THROUGH IT, IN ORDER
1 Pick the one or two leaks where value is genuinely created or destroyed — and say why those, not everything.
2 For each, ask Hammer's prior question: should this step exist in this shape at all? Decide — total redesign, or incremental improvement.
3 Sketch the redesigned flow; name what you would obliterate vs. what you would adopt as the platform standard and leave alone.
4 Write one sentence: “Our backbone fails to leverage at ___, costing ___.”
Time
Output A selective-redesign call: obliterate here, adopt-standard there. 18 min
+ present
Present: each team shows its one redesign call —this is a capstone piece, so it is presented, not just discussed.

BREAKOUT 3 · 9:37–9:58
9:37
Make it stick — and make the case
Turn the redesign into something an organization will adopt and a board will fund.
WORK THROUGH IT, IN ORDER
1 Name who inside the process must help diagnose and reshape it — start from the Frustration lens. Adoption is designed in, not bolted on.
2 Name the standardization tax your redesign imposes — whose autonomy or roles it strips — and how you would soften it.
3 Decide how you would phase the rollout: progressive vs. Big Bang, and why.
4 Write the one-line leverage case — the sentence a board would fund. Not “install X” but “redesign Y so the business changes.”
Time
Output An adoption + rollout note, and the one-line leverage case — not an installation case. 15 min
+ present
Present: each team reads its leverage case aloud —one sentence a board would fund.
